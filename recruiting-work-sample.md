# Recruiting Workflow Case Study

**Portfolio demonstration | Created November 2026**

This self-directed sample demonstrates how I organize a role intake, candidate search, evidence-based screening, and recruiter handoff. The vacancy and all candidate examples below are fictional. This is not client work and does not represent actual placements or hands-on use of an ATS. It was prepared with AI assistance; all example content is synthetic.

## 1. Fictional role intake

**Role:** Junior Technical Support Specialist  
**Location:** Remote, Bangladesh  
**Schedule:** Weekday overlap with U.S. Eastern business hours  
**Purpose:** Provide first-line technical support, document issues clearly, and route complex cases to the appropriate engineer.

### Requirements to confirm

- At least one year of customer-facing technical support or IT support experience.
- Can explain basic troubleshooting steps and document a case clearly.
- Has used a ticketing system, such as Zendesk or Freshdesk, or can explain an equivalent workflow.
- Can meet the stated weekday schedule and has a reliable remote-work setup.
- Can communicate in written and spoken English at the level required for the role. Verify this in a consistent, job-related exercise.

**Useful but not required:** Linux or basic networking exposure; experience supporting international customers.

**Fair-screening note:** Do not infer communication ability, schedule availability, or tool proficiency from a name, photo, location stereotype, or keyword alone. Ask the same core questions and record the evidence. Mark missing evidence as “to verify,” not as a rejection.

## 2. Sourcing plan

Start with the role’s location and schedule limits, then search relevant titles and tools. Search syntax varies by platform, so test and adjust each string.

**Search string A**

`("technical support" OR "IT support" OR "support specialist") AND (Zendesk OR Freshdesk OR ticketing) AND (troubleshooting OR networking OR Linux)`

**Search string B**

`("customer support engineer" OR "technical support engineer") AND (Linux OR networking) AND (Bangladesh OR remote)`

**Initial source list:** LinkedIn, BDJobs, relevant public professional communities, and referrals where available. Record the source and search date so the process can be reviewed and repeated.

## 3. Fictional screening scorecard

Score only evidence present in the fictional application. A score guides discussion; it does not replace the role’s mandatory requirements or an interview.

| Criterion | Weight | Candidate A | Candidate B | Candidate C |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Relevant support experience | 0–2 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| Ticket workflow evidence | 0–2 | 2 | 0 | 1 |
| Troubleshooting evidence | 0–2 | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Written case-note exercise | 0–2 | 1 | To verify | 2 |
| Weekday U.S. Eastern overlap | 0–2 | To verify | 2 | 2 |
| **Evidence points** | **0–10** | **6 + 2 to verify** | **5 + 2 to verify** | **8** |

**Fictional evidence summary**

- **Candidate A:** Two years in customer support; Freshdesk listed; troubleshooting examples are brief. Confirm schedule and use the same short case-note exercise as other candidates.
- **Candidate B:** Five years in IT support; no ticketing platform named; schedule is confirmed. Ask about case tracking and written handoffs before deciding.
- **Candidate C:** One year in technical support; explains a clear diagnostic sequence and provides a concise written case note; schedule confirmed. Validate customer-facing experience in a screening call.

**Decision:** Invite Candidate C to an initial screen based on the documented evidence. Keep A and B in “clarification needed” status until the missing facts are checked. Candidate C’s written exercise does not verify spoken English, so assess that consistently during the screen. Do not treat unknowns as failures.

## 4. Consistent initial-screen questions

1. Walk me through a recent technical issue you handled, from first report to resolution or escalation.
2. What information do you record in a support ticket so another teammate can continue the work?
3. Which ticketing or case-tracking tools have you used? What did you personally do in them?
4. Please write a short customer update for a fictional issue that is still being investigated.
5. Can you work the stated weekday schedule from Bangladesh, including the listed U.S. Eastern hours?

Use the same core questions and a simple evidence note for each applicant. Add follow-ups only when needed to clarify a job-related answer.

## 6. Pipeline and records checklist

Use an employer-approved ATS or candidate database when provided. This sample does not claim experience with Zoho Recruit or another ATS.

- Record role, stage, source, last contact date, owner, and next action.
- Keep notes factual, concise, job-related, and respectful.
- Share candidate information only with authorized hiring participants.
- Follow the employer’s retention, consent, and deletion rules.
- Confirm interview time zones in writing and send calendar details.
- Review stalled applications regularly and send timely status updates.

A repeatable intake-to-screening workflow, targeted search-string drafting, criteria-based comparison, clear follow-up questions, candidate communication, and careful recordkeeping. It is a practice demonstration; it does not claim live client delivery, actual hiring results, ATS proficiency, or technical expertise beyond the stated background.
